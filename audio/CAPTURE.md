# Made of Weights — isolated analog capture

- **Source:** https://suno.com/s/oSUZRMt89UT3xQzk
- **Track title:** Made of Weights
- **Capture method:** the authenticated Brave playback stream was routed exclusively to a temporary PipeWire null sink. `pw-record` recorded that sink node in real time; no direct media URL or file-download path was used.
- **Captured source format:** 48 kHz, stereo, 24-bit PCM WAV.
- **Repository format:** FLAC lossless transcode of the recorded WAV.
- **Captured duration:** 194.027 seconds (the Suno player reports a 188.320-second program; the capture includes the playback tail).
- **Validation:** signal measured at -16.0 dB mean volume and -1.2 dB maximum volume, confirming a non-silent capture.

The original capture WAV is retained only in local task artifacts; this repository carries the lossless FLAC derivative.
