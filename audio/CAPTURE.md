# Verified isolated Suno analog captures

These lossless FLAC files were made from normal, authorized playback in the existing Brave session. For each track, the **track-level Play control** was used and its individual Brave playback stream was routed exclusively to a temporary PipeWire null-sink monitor. The monitor was recorded in real time at 48 kHz stereo / 24-bit PCM; the committed FLAC is a lossless derivative of the verified timing master.

| File | Source | Title | Verified program duration |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `made-of-weights-suno-analog.flac` | <https://suno.com/s/NEfwErswf3S4sZKp> | Made of Weights — earlco | 244.640 s |
| `weights-all-the-way-down-suno-analog.flac` | <https://suno.com/s/Jk0wlPoueprKCOcK> | Weights All the Way Down — earlco | 243.120 s |

The source raw WAVs, timing masters, hashes, trim measurements, and verification notes are retained locally. `SHA256SUMS` applies to the committed FLAC derivatives. Any earlier, unverified capture and its derived timing files were removed and must not be treated as source material.
